# Uncanny Artistry homepage concept

Free homepage design concept for review. Not a live store, not a finished identity.

## Run it
Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step, no dependencies. To serve locally: `python3 -m http.server` in this folder, then visit http://localhost:8000.

## Files
- `index.html` page structure and copy (also holds the SVG sprite of concept drinkware shapes)
- `styles.css` all styling. Colors, fonts and spacing are tokens at the top of the file
- `script.js` mobile menu, placeholder buttons, join form check
- `logo.html` provisional logo direction on light, dark and lime
- `fonts/` Archivo (variable) and Permanent Marker, both SIL Open Font License, licenses included
- `assets/favicon.svg` placeholder favicon

## Version 3 final pass
- Removed the prototype notice bar, sticker, image chips, draft copy lines, the fake email form and every placeholder control (search, bag, policies, social).
- One subtle disclaimer remains in the footer: "Design concept. Product imagery is illustrative."
- Three staged product presentations (tumbler, mug, bottle), each with a lit stage, reflection, close up detail circle and a See the concept preview that opens and closes with mouse, keyboard and Escape.
- New wordmark: heavy condensed UNCANNY with one letter tilted off, a paint drip from the U, a magenta print offset and Artistry hand written on a lime swipe.
- The wordmark page is now a small identity board: lockups, monogram, drinkware mockups, color, type and clear space.
- Accessibility: pause control for the moving text and badge, focus handling in the menu and preview, no controls under 44px on touch layouts.
- Performance: deferred script, fonts preloaded when served online, no unused code, all art is inline vector with no image downloads.

## Design rationale (for the client)
- **Direction.** Black and white foundation with two controlled pops, electric lime and magenta. Graffiti appears only inside the art (paint band, drips, spray, marker notes). The layout stays clean so it feels bold without clutter.
- **Type.** Archivo at its tightest width and heaviest weight carries the headlines, large and condensed. Permanent Marker is used sparingly for hand written notes. Body text is plain Archivo for easy reading.
- **Hero.** A short headline, Everyday, Unordinary, one clear button, and a poster style stage of drinkware shapes with a glossy floor and reflections.
- **Feature section.** Three ideas in a simple list: Expression, Design, Ritual. No product or business claims.
- **Visual storytelling.** A story section with four everyday moments (morning, desk, after dark, out and about) so the page reads as a lifestyle brand, not a catalogue.
- **Growth.** The hero badge and a closing strip under the products say drinkware comes first and more follows, without naming what. New product panels are copied from one pattern in index.html.
- **Logo.** Uncanny is shown literally: the heavy word has a magenta shadow slightly out of register. Artistry is hand written on a lime swipe. Preliminary only.
- **Accessibility.** Keyboard focus rings, skip link, menu with Escape and focus handling, contrast checked on text, reduced motion respected, alt text on art.
