What it stands for

Small things, said loudly.

  • Expression

    Your cup says something before you do. We want it to say something good, and a little loud.

  • Design

    Art direction comes first. Graphic, considered and never cluttered, so everything still feels easy to live with.

  • Ritual

    A morning start, a desk refill, a late night. The everyday moments are where the brand belongs.
