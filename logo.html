Logo direction

The name is Uncanny, so the mark is slightly off on purpose. The heavy condensed top word carries a magenta shadow that sits a few pixels out of register, like a print that did not line up. Artistry is written by hand on a lime marker swipe, so the brand reads as art made on top of a product.
